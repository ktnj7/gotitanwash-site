FAQ

Common questions

How much does it cost?

Every job is different, so we price by size and condition. Send 2–3 photos and your zip code and we'll give you an exact price within the hour, no obligation.

Will pressure washing damage my roof or walls?

We never use high pressure on roofs, brick, stucco or paint. We soft wash: low pressure plus a cleaning solution that kills mold and algae at the root, so it gets cleaner and stays clean longer.

Is it safe for my plants and pets?

Yes. We pre-wet your plants, rinse them afterward, and ask that pets stay inside while we work.

Do I need to be home?

No. We just need access to an outdoor water spigot and the work area. We'll text when we arrive and send photos when we're done.

Do you work with commercial properties and HOAs?

Yes. We clean storefronts, sidewalks, parking areas, dumpster pads and common areas, and can work early mornings or after hours.
